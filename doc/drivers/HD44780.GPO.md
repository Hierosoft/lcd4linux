# HD44780 GPO

Extension of the LCD display by 8 outputs (GPOs)

## Requirements
* 1 Stk. 74HC573
* 1 Stk. ULN2803
* 8 Stk. RES 330R
* 8 Stk. LED rot 3mm

## Circuit diagram
The circuit is connected parallel to the display.
- INIT is pin 16 of the LPT on the computer.
```
---|330r|--- This should be a 330 ohm resistor,
             but you can also use 470 ohms.
  ---|<---   That's supposed to be an LED.
    Vcc      Supply voltage 5V DC
    GND      Ground

           74HC573
        ______  ______              ULN2803
        |    |__|    |           ______  ______
 GND o--|1         20|---+ Vcc   |    |__|    |
  D0 o--|2         19|-----------|1         18|----|330r|---|<---+ Vcc
  D1 o--|3         18|-----------|2         17|----|330r|---|<---+ Vcc
  D2 o--|4         17|-----------|3         16|----|330r|---|<---+ Vcc
  D3 o--|5         16|-----------|4         15|----|330r|---|<---+ Vcc
  D4 o--|6         15|-----------|5         14|----|330r|---|<---+ Vcc
  D5 o--|7         14|-----------|6         13|----|330r|---|<---+ Vcc
  D6 o--|8         13|-----------|7         12|----|330r|---|<---+ Vcc
  D7 o--|9         12|-----------|8         11|----|330r|---|<---+ Vcc
INIT o--|11        10|--o GND o--|9         10|----+ Vcc
        --------------           --------------
```

Instead of LEDs, you can also control something else.
The ULN2803 has an open collector output that can be loaded with 30V.
But be careful!!!

The entire circuit consumes a maximum of approximately 130mA at 5V DC.
I take the voltage from the game port.
It works for me, but I don't offer any guarantees.
